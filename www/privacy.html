Number Pop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Number Pop is a math game for children and anyone who enjoys calm, colorful play. It is made by [YOUR NAME]. This page explains what the game does and does not do with information.

We collect nothing

  • The game does not ask for a name, email, age, photo or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat.
  • The game does not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and it does not share any information with anyone.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

What stays on the device

The number of stars earned is saved only on the device the game is played on, so it is still there next time. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the game deletes it.

Spoken words

Problems and answers are read aloud by the device's own built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded, and no audio leaves the device.

Children

Number Pop is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. This follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's rules of Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
